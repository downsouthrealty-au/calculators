WA general duty rates (from RevenueWA)
General information only, not financial, tax or legal advice. Transfer duty (stamp duty) uses the WA general rate (Duties Fact Sheet Residential Land, wa.gov.au, updated 5 March 2026) and the first home owner rate of duty for contracts on or after 7 May 2026 (wa.gov.au first home owner rate fact sheet, updated 27 August 2026; 2026-27 Housing Taxation Package, 23 Oct 2026). First home owner rates are shown only when the price is within the limits ($800,000 home, $550,000 vacant land) and you must meet the eligibility rules, including the other conditions for the first home owner rate. Other concessions, for example for pensioners, are not included. Check your own position with RevenueWA (revenue.wa.gov.au). The duty is on the dutiable value, which is usually the price. Landgate registration fees and other costs are not included. Rates change, last checked 24 Nov 2026.
