Sell and buy calculator (WA)

Selling one property and buying another? Work out what you need to sell for to afford the next one.

Selling costs (editable)
Defaults are from the DownSouth Realty Sales Information Booklet (GST included). Leave a field empty to leave it out.
Buying costs (editable)
Transfer duty uses the WA general rate on the purchase price and is worked out for you. Add your own estimate of the other costs. Leave a field empty to leave it out.
New loan repayments (optional)
Enter the price you want to buy for to see what you need to sell for.
General information only, not financial, tax or legal advice. This works out the sale price you would need so that the money from selling, plus your new loan and savings, covers the purchase price, transfer duty and the other costs you enter. Transfer duty uses the WA general rate (Duties Fact Sheet Residential Land, wa.gov.au, updated 5 March 2026), so concessions and the first home owner rate are not included. Selling costs default to the DownSouth Realty Sales Information Booklet and can be edited. Capital gains tax, land tax, loan approval, lender mortgage insurance and bridging finance are not included. It assumes both settlements line up. Check your own position with your lender, conveyancer and accountant. Rates change, last checked 25 Nov 2026.
