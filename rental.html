WA rules checked 23 Nov 2026 against Consumer Protection WA (consumerprotection.wa.gov.au/rental-bonds, updated 28 March 2026; BondLodgementform0626.pdf, June 2026): a landlord can ask for up to 4 weeks' rent as a bond (no limit where rent is over $1,200 a week), up to 2 weeks' rent in advance, and a $350 pet bond. The income comparison uses a common rule of thumb that rent should be about 30% or less of gross income. It is not a legal test and lenders and property managers set their own criteria. Actual bond and advance amounts are set in your lease.
