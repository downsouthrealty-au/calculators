General information only, not financial, tax or legal advice. Transfer duty uses the WA general rate on the dutiable value, which includes GST when GST is payable (wa.gov.au, Transfer duty assessment, updated 30 July 2026). GST: the sale of commercial premises is generally taxable, with the margin scheme or going concern rules in some cases, and a buyer registered for GST can usually claim the GST back as a credit (ato.gov.au, Selling commercial premises). Tell your accountant which applies. There is no first home owner grant or first home buyer scheme for commercial property. Land tax uses the WA rates on the unimproved land value (wa.gov.au, Land tax assessment, updated 20 May 2026): nil to $300,000, $300 from $300,001 to $420,000, then $300 plus 0.25 cents per dollar over $420,000 to $1,000,000, $1,750 plus 0.9 cents per dollar over $1,000,000 to $1,800,000, and higher rates above that. Mortgage registration is Landgate's fee from 1 July 2026. Landgate's transfer registration fee is not included. Landlord fees default to the DownSouth Realty Commercial PM Price List (GST inclusive, from 16 Nov 2026) and every fee can be changed; the other-agency figures are entered by you and are not checked by DownSouth Realty. Lease terms, outgoings, security and rent reviews are set in your lease. Repayments are principal and interest at a fixed rate, for illustration. Figures are estimates, not a quote, and are before income tax.
