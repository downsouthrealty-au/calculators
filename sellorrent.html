This compares selling today with renting the property out and selling at the end of the years you choose, using only the numbers you enter. It does not know future prices, rents, rates or returns, so growth and return assumptions start at 0%. Selling costs default to the DownSouth Realty Sales Information Booklet (commission 2.5%, photography $300, online marketing $900, GST included) and are applied again if you sell at the end. Letting costs default to the DownSouth Realty Residential PM Price List (management 8.5%, leasing 1.1 weeks, marketing $210, photography $200, condition report $200, final bond $200, routine inspection $70, admin $15 a month, annual summary $55, GST included). Letting costs for a new letting apply in the first year only. The mortgage is treated as interest only, for illustration. Tax (including capital gains tax and land tax exemptions), lenders mortgage insurance and your own circumstances are not included: ask your accountant. Last checked 24 Nov 2026.
