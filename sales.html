General information only, not financial, tax or legal advice. Transfer duty uses the WA general rate (Duties Fact Sheet Residential Land, wa.gov.au, updated 5 March 2026) and the first home owner rate of duty for contracts on or after 7 May 2026 (wa.gov.au first home owner rate fact sheet, updated 27 August 2026; 2026-27 Housing Taxation Package, 23 Oct 2026). First home owner duty rates are shown only when the price is within the limits ($800,000 home, $550,000 vacant land) and you must meet the eligibility rules. Check with RevenueWA (revenue.wa.gov.au). Mortgage registration is Landgate's $225.10 fee from 1 July 2026. Landgate's transfer registration fee depends on the price and is not included: use Landgate's Transfer Lodgement Fee Calculator. The first home owner grant is not included. Repayments are principal and interest at a fixed rate, for illustration. Ask your lender for a real quote. Rates and thresholds change, last checked 23 Nov 2026.
