General information only, not financial, tax or legal advice. Transfer duty uses the WA general rate (Duties Fact Sheet Residential Land, wa.gov.au, updated 5 March 2026) and the first home owner rate of duty for contracts on or after 7 May 2026 (wa.gov.au first home owner rate fact sheet, updated 27 August 2026; 2026-27 Housing Taxation Package, 23 Oct 2026). First home owner duty rates are shown only when the price is within the limits ($800,000 home, $550,000 vacant land) and you must meet the eligibility rules. Check with RevenueWA (revenue.wa.gov.au). Mortgage registration is Landgate's $225.10 fee from 1 July 2026. Landgate's transfer registration fee depends on the price and is not included: use Landgate's Transfer Lodgement Fee Calculator. Grants and schemes: the first home owner grant (FHOG) is up to $10,000 for first home buyers of new homes, up to $800,000 south of the 26th parallel, no income test (wa.gov.au About the first home owner grant, updated 29 July 2026). Australian Government 5% Deposit Scheme price caps: $850,000 capital city, $600,000 other areas WA, no income cap (firsthomebuyers.gov.au, Property Price Caps and First Home Buyers pages). Help to Buy: minimum 2% deposit, Government contribution up to 40% for new homes and 30% for existing homes, income limit $103,000 single and $165,000 joint for 2026-27, same price caps (firsthomebuyers.gov.au, Help to Buy pages and 2026-27 thresholds). Keystart Low Deposit Home Loan: price limit $860,000, income limit $155,000 single and $228,000 couples and families, deposit the higher of 2% or $2,000 (keystart.com.au, 15 April 2026 product settings and Low Deposit Home Loan page). These are guides only. Your lender, Housing Australia, Keystart and RevenueWA decide eligibility, and you can use only one of the Government 5% Deposit Scheme, Help to Buy or a state loan such as Keystart at a time. Check the price cap for your suburb and postcode with the Housing Australia postcode tool. Repayments are principal and interest at a fixed rate, for illustration. Ask your lender for a real quote. Rates and thresholds change, last checked 23 Nov 2026.
