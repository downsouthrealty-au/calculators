Rental income is shown before tax. Added estimates (checked 23 Nov 2026). Every fee above can be edited; defaults are the DownSouth Realty Residential PM Price List (GST inclusive). Land tax uses the WA land tax rates on the aggregated unimproved value of the land you own in the same name (wa.gov.au, Land tax assessment, updated 20 May 2026): nil to $300,000, $300 from $300,001 to $420,000, then $300 plus 0.25 cents per dollar over $420,000 to $1,000,000, $1,750 plus 0.9 cents per dollar over $1,000,000 to $1,800,000, and higher rates above that. Land tax is on land value, not the home, uses the Valuer-General unimproved value (Landgate), is based on ownership at 30 June, does not cover land held in trust or by a company (assessed separately), and your own exemptions or concessions are not applied. Check with RevenueWA. The maintenance allowance (default 1% of property value a year), landlord insurance and other costs are your own estimates.
