General information only, not financial, tax or legal advice. This compares the cash cost of buying and renting over the years you choose, using only the numbers you enter. It does not know future prices, rents, rates or returns, so growth and return assumptions start at 0%. It treats the cash a renter does not spend on buying, including the deposit, duty and any yearly saving against the owner's costs, as invested at the return you enter. It does not include tax, rental or negative gearing effects, lifestyle, or the value of owning. Transfer duty uses the WA general rate (Duties Fact Sheet Residential Land, wa.gov.au, updated 5 March 2026) and the first home owner rate for contracts on or after 7 May 2026 (wa.gov.au first home owner rate fact sheet, updated 27 August 2026), and you must meet the eligibility rules. Mortgage registration is Landgate's $225.10 fee from 1 July 2026. The first home owner grant, other schemes, lenders mortgage insurance and Landgate's transfer registration fee are not included. Repayments are principal and interest at a fixed rate, for illustration. Ask your lender for a real quote. Last checked 24 Nov 2026.
