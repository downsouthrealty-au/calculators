Rent affordability calculator (WA)

Work out how much rent you can afford from your income, and the cash needed to move in.

30% or less of gross income is a common rule of thumb for affordable rent. It is not a legal test. Lenders and property managers set their own criteria.
Enter your income to see the rent range.
General information only, not financial or legal advice. The rent share uses a common rule of thumb that rent should be about 30% or less of gross income (before tax). Lenders and property managers set their own criteria, and your other costs and savings matter too. Bond and advance rules checked 24 Nov 2026 against Consumer Protection WA (consumerprotection.wa.gov.au/rental-bonds, updated 28 March 2026; BondLodgementform0626.pdf, June 2026): a landlord can ask for up to 4 weeks' rent as a bond (no limit where rent is over $1,200 a week), up to 2 weeks' rent in advance, and a $350 pet bond. Actual bond and advance amounts are set in your lease. Figures are estimates from the numbers you enter.
