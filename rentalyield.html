Enter a price and weekly rent to see the yield.
General information only, not financial, tax or legal advice. Gross yield is the yearly rent divided by the price. Net yield takes off the costs you enter and the management fee. Management and admin default to the DownSouth Realty residential price list (management 8.5% of rent collected, admin $15 a month, GST included). New letting costs such as the leasing fee, marketing, photography, lease renewals, routine inspections and the annual summary are not included here: use the landlord net-return calculator for those. WA land tax uses the scale on wa.gov.au (Land tax assessment, updated 20 May 2026) for a single owner and your land value, and a land tax figure is only a guide: exemptions and ownership structure change it. Mortgage interest and tax are not included. Yields are estimates from the numbers you enter. Last checked 24 Nov 2026.
