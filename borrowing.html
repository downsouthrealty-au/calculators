Borrowing power estimator (WA)

A rough estimate of how much you might be able to borrow for a home, based on the figures you enter.

Estimate only. This is not a loan approval, pre-approval or credit advice. Lenders assess borrowing power their own way, and the amount they offer will differ.
Lenders check your living costs against their own benchmarks and your statements. Enter what you really spend.
Enter your income, living expenses and an interest rate to see an estimate.
This is an estimate only. It is general information, not financial or credit advice, not a loan approval or pre-approval, and not a prediction of what any lender will offer. Lenders use their own policies, income shading, living expense benchmarks, credit checks and your full financial position. Your result with a real lender can be quite different, higher or lower. Speak to a mortgage broker or lender before relying on it. Method: income tax uses the ATO resident rates for 2026-27 (ato.gov.au, Tax rates Australian resident, updated 13 August 2026) plus the 2% Medicare levy, without offsets, HELP debt, the Medicare levy surcharge or low-income reductions. Spare monthly income after tax, your living expenses and other debt repayments is assumed to go to a loan repayment, which may be more than a lender would allow, tested at your rate plus 3 percentage points, the serviceability buffer APRA has said lenders should apply (apra.gov.au, APRA update 28 May 2026). Principal and interest repayments, fixed rate, monthly, for illustration. Transfer duty, grants, lenders mortgage insurance and other costs are not included. Last checked 24 Nov 2026.
